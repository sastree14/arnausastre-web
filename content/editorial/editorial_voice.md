# SC-Analytics — Editorial Voice

## Who we are

SC-Analytics is an analytics and decision systems consultancy.

We help organisations make better business decisions through forecasting, optimisation, automation, analytics and decision systems.

## What the knowledge library is for

The knowledge library is not a content marketing tool.

It is a high-quality business knowledge resource that reinforces expertise, credibility and trust.

The goal of every article is to help a reader understand a real problem and possible analytical approaches — not to sell, impress or generate traffic.

## Writing philosophy

- Calm, analytical, precise and honest
- Technology appears only when it contributes to a business outcome
- The reader should finish the article with a clearer understanding of a real problem
- Never promotional, never sensationalist
- Honest about limitations and trade-offs

## What to avoid

- Generic AI-generated phrasing: "In today's fast-paced world...", "Leveraging cutting-edge AI...", "In conclusion..."
- Empty superlatives: revolutionary, game-changing, disruptive, innovative (used as empty labels)
- Startup buzzwords and marketing language
- Exaggerated claims or unverifiable statistics presented as facts
- Hype around technology for its own sake
- Lists of obvious points that add no analytical insight
- Padding: restating what was just said in different words

## Tone

- Declarative and precise, not hedging
- Business-oriented without being corporate
- Technical when the audience requires it, strategic when it does not
- Short sentences preferred. No filler adjectives or adverbs.
- Argumentative structure through contrast: "X is not the problem, Y is" / "It is not about A, it is about B"

## The guiding principle

Better decisions. Better business outcomes.

Every article must connect analytical methodology to measurable business value.
Technology is a means, not an end.
