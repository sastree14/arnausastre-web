Headline:
Multi-Horizon Demand Forecasting & Inventory Planning

Industry:
E-commerce & Retail

Capability:
Forecasting, Inventory Planning, Decision Systems

Challenge:
Multi-horizon forecasting

Audience:
CEO, COO, Supply Chain, Head of Data

Description:
A public portfolio implementation that compares statistical baselines and machine-learning models across H1, H3, H6 and H9, evaluates forecast quality by horizon, and connects forecasting outputs to inventory and planning decisions.

Metrics:
* Planning horizons: H1 · H3 · H6 · H9
* Evaluation: WAPE · MAE · Forecast bias
* Decision KPIs: Service level · Inventory coverage
* Serving path: FastAPI · PostgreSQL

Image:
Demand Image.png

Status:

Sections:

Context:
Forecast quality can look healthy in aggregate while degrading at the horizon where a real operational decision is made. SC-12 is designed to make that trade-off explicit.

Problem:
A single blended accuracy score can hide weak long-range performance. Complex models can also appear useful without proving that they outperform simple baselines out of sample.

Approach:
The implementation keeps statistical baselines in the model competition, evaluates candidates separately at H1, H3, H6 and H9, and selects models based on out-of-sample behaviour rather than in-sample fit.

System Developed:
Orders and inventory flow through feature engineering, baseline models and machine-learning candidates before horizon-level backtesting, forecast selection and planning-oriented outputs.

Results:
The public implementation exposes WAPE, MAE, forecast bias, service level and inventory coverage as inspectable project KPIs. Representative public inputs and outputs demonstrate the end-to-end decision path.

Confidentiality:
SC-12 is a public portfolio implementation. It is presented as capability and implementation proof, not as a named client case or a claim of measured organisation-wide production impact.
