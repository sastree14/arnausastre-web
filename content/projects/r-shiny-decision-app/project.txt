Context:
Analytical work often stops at a notebook, spreadsheet or slide deck. The model may be sound, but if a manager has to ask an analyst to rerun it every time an assumption changes, the analysis is not yet an operational system.

Problem:
The team needs direct access to scenarios and outputs without exposing implementation complexity or allowing accidental changes to the underlying methodology.

Approach:
The application separates model logic, data preparation and interface components. Users interact with a small number of meaningful business controls while the statistical calculations remain reproducible and versioned.

System Developed:
The Shiny interface combines interactive inputs, scenario comparisons, statistical outputs and exportable management views. The same analytical engine can be extended as new data or decisions are introduced.

Results:
The result converts a repeatable analytical process into a tool that can be used directly by non-technical stakeholders while preserving the rigor of the underlying R workflow.
