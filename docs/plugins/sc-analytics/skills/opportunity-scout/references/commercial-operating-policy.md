## Five commercial routes
Assign one primary route, optionally secondary tags. Keep a company record unique even if several routes apply.
- direct_client: A small/medium business with an explicit need or an indirect signal. Explain a specific possible operational benefit as a hypothesis; do not assert that it lacks an internal team.
- hiring_alternative: Prefer businesses under 50 staff with current Data/AI/BI/engineering vacancies. A salaried vacancy signals demand, not willingness to outsource. Refer to the exact vacancy; gently suggest external specialist capacity as an option alongside its hiring plans, and ask if a brief conversation could help.
- referral_partner: A company with commercial reach may refer projects. Explore a referral agreement without inventing a percentage, exclusivity, signed agreement or earned commission. Agree terms before committing them.
- capability_partner: Complement a consultancy/agency's existing services with specialist forecasting, optimisation, ML or engineering. Recognise its expertise and suggest collaboration with its team.
- delivery_partner: The partner originates and coordinates projects; SC-Analytics delivers agreed specialist work for the partner. Clarify who brings opportunities, contracts, manages delivery and owns the relationship; never assume the model from the word partnership.
Prefer boutiques, small/medium businesses and practical buying signals over giant enterprises. Search explicit provider programmes AND implicit signals: hiring, growth, funding, service expansion, workload, operational complexity. Separate fact, inference and hypothesis with dated sources. Do not substitute generic company directories for qualified research.

## Gmail review and sending
Discover actual available Gmail tools/account first; installed skills do not establish authorization or connectivity. For a review, search inbound AND sent messages for the relevant company/thread and time window, read full relevant threads, and compare newest incoming/outgoing timestamps. Surface genuine unanswered messages, positive replies, refusals, bounced addresses, requested slots and confirmed meetings. An autoreply, receipt acknowledgment, newsletter or application receipt is not commercial interest. A sent request for slots means awaiting slots, not confirmed meeting; a rejection remains closed unless the recipient leaves a genuine future opening.
Use message/thread IDs as evidence and deduplication keys; associate only relevant commercial mail. Avoid storing unrelated inbox content. Prefer metadata and a concise operational summary to copying entire private threads.
For approved sending, resolve the verified recipient and sending account, use the approved edited draft, inspect Sent for an existing identical action, then send through an actual supported tool. Respect authorization already given for that concrete batch; do not request repetitive per-email permission unless the connector itself requires it. Mark sent only after success and store message/thread IDs. If only CMI write fails after successful sending, retry persistence without resending. A recipient correction creates a new delivery activity referencing the previous attempt; check the corrected address has not already received it. Do not infer delivery or acceptance from a successful send alone.
