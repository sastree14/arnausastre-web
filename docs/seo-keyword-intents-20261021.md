## 3. Editorial linking rules

1. Map each article to **one relevant service**, rather than linking every article to the same generic page.
2. Keep a separate route to a **specific, substantiated case**, when an appropriate public case exists.
3. Preserve the contact action and current three-card design.
4. Never add links to unpublished CMI target paths.
5. Use localized, descriptive anchor text; avoid exact-match keyword repetition.
6. Monitor whether the service CTA attracts qualified visitors rather than optimizing only click volume.

The first mapping is coded in `lib/seo-clusters.ts` and is used by both static and automatically generated article templates.

## 4. Measurement and decisions

The initial source of evidence is Search Console (queries, pages, impressions, clicks and indexation); GA4 measures consented on-site interactions, and the CRM tracks qualified leads.

Before adding any new pages, confirm:
- Customer job-to-be-done and whether the query implies buying consulting services.
- Distinct content and decision/problem model; no doorway-page cloning.
- Actual case studies that can be referenced truthfully.
- SEO title/meta description and language-specific content.
- Internal links from and to relevant knowledge articles.
- One appropriate contact/discovery CTA.
- Technical QA and canonical/sitemap validation.

## 5. What is deliberately excluded from this implementation slice

- No autogenerated new landing pages and no bulk upload to Supabase.
- No purchased keywords, rank tracking or AI content packages.
- No link exchange or auto-submission to directories.
- No full site i18n rewrite.
- No changes to design tokens, CSS, hero layout or visual page structure.
