# SC-Analytics — Acquisition Channels

The Growth Agent supports three acquisition engines.

## Marketplaces

Use marketplaces for immediate opportunities and strategic long-term accounts. Prioritize credible clients, verified payment/history where available, important business problems and repeat potential. Profitable one-off work is acceptable when strategically sensible.

## Direct Sales

Target selected companies whose operations reveal a concrete analytical or AI opportunity. Outreach must be personalized and problem-led. Do not send generic Data Science pitches. The first objective is a relevant business conversation, not an immediate proposal.

## Partnerships

This is a strategic priority. Look for adjacent firms with overlapping clients but complementary capabilities, including BI consultancies, ERP integrators, software agencies, finance consultancies, fractional CFOs, operations/supply-chain consultancies, specialized recruiters and analytics firms lacking advanced forecasting/optimization/ML.

Potential relationship models:

- Referral commission
- Channel partner
- White-label / subcontract
- Co-delivery
- Mutual referrals

A strong partnership candidate has credible access to the target market, complementary rather than directly duplicative capabilities, a clear reason to involve SC-Analytics, and the possibility of recurring opportunity flow.
