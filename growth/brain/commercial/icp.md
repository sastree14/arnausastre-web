# SC-Analytics — Ideal Client Profiles

Primary direct-sales ICP:

- Established SMEs and lower-mid-market companies, often 10–250 employees.
- Traditional companies with ERP/Excel/manual planning and meaningful operational complexity.
- Scale-ups whose planning, analytics or operating processes have outgrown informal tools.
- Companies without a full internal Data/AI team that need an external partner.
- Companies with an analyst or technical team that need advanced forecasting, optimization, AI or specialist capacity.

High-signal business situations:

- Forecasting or planning is manual, short-horizon or unreliable.
- Inventory, capacity, scheduling, resource allocation or pricing decisions have measurable economic impact.
- Reporting consumes substantial manual effort and does not support decisions well.
- Data exists across ERP/CRM/BI/spreadsheets but is not converted into operational decisions.
- Repetitive analytical workflows may benefit from bounded AI automation.
- The company is scaling and existing decision processes are no longer adequate.

Preferred decision makers:

- Founder / CEO
- COO / Operations Director
- CFO / Finance Director
- Supply Chain / Planning Director
- Head of Data / Analytics where specialist support is needed

Avoid qualifying solely on company size. A candidate must have a plausible decision problem, economic value, access path and realistic ability to buy external services.
