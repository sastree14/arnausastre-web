# SC-Analytics — Identity

SC-Analytics is a Data & AI consultancy focused on improving business decisions, processes and results through forecasting, mathematical optimization, machine learning, analytics, automation and decision systems.

Core idea: **Comprender antes de construir.**

Technology is a tool, not the starting point. The work begins with understanding the business, the decision to improve, the constraints, the available data and the measurable value of solving the problem.

SC-Analytics aims to be a close, trusted external Data & AI partner rather than a one-off technical supplier.

Internal identity chain:

CONFIANZA → COMPRENSIÓN → VALOR → RIGOR → RELACIÓN A LARGO PLAZO

Internal phrase:

> Queremos recuperar la confianza de la vieja escuela utilizando las herramientas del futuro.
