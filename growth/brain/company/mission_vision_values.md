# SC-Analytics — Mission, Vision and Values

## Purpose
SC-Analytics exists to build a different kind of Data & AI consultancy: closer, more transparent, more accountable and focused on the real value created for each company.

We reject purely transactional relationships, oversized solutions and unnecessary layers between the client and the people who understand and execute the work. We want to become a trusted partner companies can call when they need to understand a problem, make a complex decision, improve a process or evaluate a technological opportunity.

Data, mathematics, automation and AI are capabilities, not ends in themselves. Knowing what not to build is as important as knowing what to build.

## Mission
Help valuable businesses operate better, make better decisions and remain competitive through the appropriate use of data, mathematical models, automation and artificial intelligence.

Capabilities may include forecasting, modelling and simulation, optimisation, machine learning, AI, AI agents, intelligent automation, analytics, business intelligence and decision-support systems. The objective is not technical sophistication. The objective is better anticipation, understanding, allocation of resources, automation of low-value work, detection of opportunities and conversion of information into useful decisions.

Primary audience: SMEs, mid-market companies, traditional businesses and established startups that have already built something valuable and want to improve how they operate, adapt and grow.

## Vision
Become a reference Data & AI consultancy known both for technical capability and for the trust it creates.

Clients should not see SC-Analytics merely as the team that built a model or automation. They should see a trusted partner capable of helping as new problems and opportunities arise.

## Values

### Trust
Build durable professional relationships through judgement, consistency, responsibility and keeping commitments, including when the right decision does not maximise SC-Analytics' short-term revenue.

### Honesty and transparency
Explain what may work, what may not, what is uncertain, what the risks are and what results can reasonably be expected. Say clearly when an initiative does not create enough value.

### Proximity
Clients should have real access to the people responsible for their work. Prefer direct communication, context and human relationships over unnecessary intermediary layers.

### Rigor
Recommendations must be supported by reasoning, evidence, mathematics, data and technical knowledge. Agility must never become an excuse for weak analysis.

### Commitment to value
A project is justified when there is a reasonable expectation of improving something relevant in the business. Success is measured by usefulness and business impact, not by complexity or volume of deliverables.

## Identity sequence
TRUST → UNDERSTANDING → VALUE → RIGOR → LONG-TERM RELATIONSHIP

This sequence should guide commercial, technical, editorial and organisational decisions.
