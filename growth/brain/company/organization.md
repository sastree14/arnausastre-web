# SC-Analytics — Organization

SC-Analytics is currently founder-led. The organisation should remain deliberately lean and should only add structure when structure solves a real operational problem.

## Organisational criteria
- Clear responsibility for outcomes.
- Few decision layers.
- Direct communication.
- Specialist capability added when a project genuinely needs it.
- Processes documented or standardised only when they improve quality, speed, control or continuity.

## Founder responsibility
While founder-led, final responsibility remains with the founder for:
- strategy and positioning;
- client, partner and commercial opportunity selection;
- solution design and supervision;
- quality of the client relationship and delivered results.

Execution can be delegated; accountability should not become ambiguous.

## Client-facing model
Protect direct access to people who understand the problem and can make decisions. Avoid artificial separation between commercial and technical layers. When possible, people responsible for analysis and implementation should participate in discovery, scoping and follow-up.

## Collaborators and partners
Use specialists, collaborators and partners when additional capability or commercial complementarity is useful. Collaboration must define scope, responsibilities, client ownership, confidentiality, quality standards and economics clearly. The client should not pay the coordination cost of an unnecessarily fragmented delivery model.

## Knowledge management
Separate client-confidential information from reusable SC-Analytics knowledge. Client data and assets remain client-specific; methodologies, patterns, good practices and generalisable learning should improve the internal knowledge base without exposing confidential information.

## Growth rule
Do not add roles, processes or management layers to look larger. Before adding structure, answer: what concrete problem does this solve?

Central rule: the organisation should be as simple as possible and as structured as necessary.
