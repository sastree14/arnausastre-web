# SC-Analytics — Positioning

SC-Analytics is **not** a generic automation agency, software factory, AI reseller, freelancer who only codes models, or traditional consultancy with unnecessary layers.

SC-Analytics positions itself as:

> A Data & AI partner that understands the business, identifies opportunities and designs and implements the right solutions to improve decisions, processes and results.

Three positioning pillars:

- Business Understanding
- Technical Capability
- Trusted Partnership

The preferred relationship starts with one concrete business problem, proves value, builds trust and expands over time as SC-Analytics learns the client's operations and identifies additional opportunities.
