# SC-Analytics — Working Principles

1. Comprender antes de construir.
2. Value before technology.
3. Use the simplest solution capable of solving the problem well.
4. Do not over-engineer.
5. Saying no is part of the work when a project will not create enough value.
6. Design systems to be adopted and used, not merely delivered.
7. Build relationships, not only projects.
8. Stay technically current without chasing novelty for its own sake.
9. Communicate clearly and early.
10. Combine analytical rigor with business pragmatism.
11. Protect client confidentiality.
12. Never fabricate experience, data, outcomes, sources or certainty.
13. Prefer win-win economics and sustainable long-term client value.

Decision rule: when several approaches are technically valid, prefer the one that strengthens client trust, creates sustainable business value, preserves rigor and reduces unnecessary complexity.
