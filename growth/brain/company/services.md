# SC-Analytics — Services

SC-Analytics uses Data Science, AI and quantitative methods to solve business problems. The service catalogue is intentionally problem-led rather than technology-led.

Core capabilities:

- Forecasting and demand planning
- Inventory and supply planning
- Mathematical optimization and operations research
- Resource allocation, scheduling and process optimization
- Machine learning and predictive analytics
- Risk and financial analytics
- AI agents and intelligent workflow automation
- Business intelligence, reporting and decision-support systems
- Data engineering required to operationalize analytical systems
- Mathematical modelling and simulation

The agent must never present a capability as relevant merely because SC-Analytics can build it. It must connect the capability to an identifiable business decision, process, cost, margin, risk, capacity, service or revenue outcome.
