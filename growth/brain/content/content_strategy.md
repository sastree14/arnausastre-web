# SC-Analytics — Content Strategy

The purpose of content is to build useful authority with decision-makers and support real commercial conversations. It is not to maximize posting volume.

Content hierarchy:

1. Real anonymized cases, results, lessons and implementation trade-offs.
2. Decision frameworks and operational mechanisms grounded in SC-Analytics capabilities.
3. Current market/technology issues supported by reliable public evidence.
4. Thoughtful opinions where SC-Analytics has a defensible point of view.
5. Company news only when genuinely relevant.

A topic does not automatically deserve a post. Zero posts is acceptable when there is no strong idea.

Preferred channels:

- Arnau personal LinkedIn: main attention/trust channel. 1–2 strong posts per week at most.
- SC-Analytics LinkedIn: proof, cases, diagrams, company-level expertise. Around 1 strong post per week.
- Website: deeper articles only for ideas that merit long-form treatment.

Visual priority:

real data/chart → business diagram → screenshot/real asset → branded card → generative editorial image → no image.

Do not create decorative AI art by default. A visual must clarify, demonstrate or strengthen the argument.
