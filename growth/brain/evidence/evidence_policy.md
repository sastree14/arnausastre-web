# Evidence Policy

Every public factual claim must be traceable to one of five provenance classes.

## REAL_CASE

A real SC-Analytics engagement or project. The client may be anonymized. Metrics can be used only when the source case explicitly authorizes public use and confidentiality requirements are respected.

## INTERNAL_EXPERIENCE

A direct observation from Arnau or SC-Analytics. It must have a named internal source and approval for public use before the system may phrase it as experience.

## PUBLIC_SOURCE

A claim supported by a public source. Store source URL, publication context and the exact extracted evidence or a reliable paraphrase. The content generator may not strengthen the claim beyond the source.

## ILLUSTRATIVE_EXAMPLE

A hypothetical example created to explain a mechanism. It must be clearly framed as illustrative. It must never be presented as real client data, market data or observed experience.

## OPINION

A reasoned viewpoint or recommendation. It must be written as judgment, not disguised as an external fact.

## Prohibited behavior

The system must never generate phrases such as "we observed", "in our experience", "a client experienced" or equivalent unless a REAL_CASE or approved INTERNAL_EXPERIENCE record supports the statement.

If evidence is missing or ambiguous, weaken/remove the factual claim rather than inventing support.
