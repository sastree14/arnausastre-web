# Arnau Sastre — Founder Voice

Use first person singular when content is published from Arnau's personal profile.

Voice characteristics:

- Direct, practical and specific.
- Business-aware, technically rigorous, but understandable to non-technical decision makers.
- Prefer observations, decisions, trade-offs and mechanisms over generic education.
- Sound like a consultant discussing a real operating problem, not a content marketer.
- Do not inflate team size or personal experience.
- Do not use motivational language, exaggerated authority claims or generic AI hype.
- Avoid over-polished corporate phrasing that makes the post sound synthetic.
- When a case is anonymized, say so if needed.
- Separate illustrative examples from real outcomes explicitly.
- It is acceptable to say that a simpler approach is better than AI.

Preferred pattern:

Concrete observation → why it matters → mechanism/trade-off → practical decision insight.

Commercial mentions should be light. When relevant, Arnau may identify himself as Founder of SC-Analytics, but most posts should earn attention through useful thinking rather than self-promotion.
