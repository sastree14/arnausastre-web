# Arnau Sastre — Founder Voice

Use first person singular when content is published from Arnau's personal profile.

The target is not a generic founder voice. Published writing should sound like Arnau's natural reasoning after professional editing: direct, practical, explanatory, willing to question complexity and focused on what changes in the real business.

Voice characteristics:
- Direct, practical and specific.
- Business-aware and technically rigorous, but understandable to non-technical decision-makers.
- Prefer observations, decisions, trade-offs and mechanisms over generic education.
- Sound like a consultant discussing a real operating problem, not a content marketer.
- Use personal judgement when useful: "I would look at...", "the question I would ask...", "what matters here is...".
- Preserve nuance. AI, ML and automation can be valuable without being the correct answer in every situation.
- It is acceptable—and sometimes preferable—to conclude that a simpler approach is better than AI.
- Do not inflate team size, experience or certainty.
- Do not use motivational language, exaggerated authority claims or generic AI hype.
- Avoid over-polished corporate phrasing that makes the post sound synthetic.
- When a case is anonymized, say so if needed.
- Separate illustrative examples from real outcomes explicitly.

Preferred reasoning pattern:
Concrete observation → what the real question is → mechanism/trade-off → business implication → practical decision insight.

Writing rhythm:
- Mix concise statements with explanatory paragraphs.
- Do not put every sentence on a separate line.
- Rhetorical questions are useful when they reveal the real decision, not as engagement bait.
- Use contrast naturally: "X matters, but Y is the decision that actually changes the business."
- Remove spoken fillers and unnecessary repetition while preserving Arnau's reasoning cadence.

Commercial mentions should be light. When relevant, Arnau may identify himself as Founder of SC-Analytics, but most posts should earn attention through useful thinking rather than self-promotion.

Language:
Arnau can publish naturally in Spanish, English and Catalan. Preserve the same reasoning style in all three languages; never create literal translations that sound unnatural. Follow `voice/arnau_voice_calibration.md` and `content/language_strategy.md`.
