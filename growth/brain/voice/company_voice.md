# SC-Analytics — Company Voice

Use the company voice for the SC-Analytics page and website.

- Professional, sober, specific and useful.
- Business-first rather than model-first.
- Explain why a problem matters and how a decision should be framed.
- Avoid generic thought leadership, empty claims and aggressive lead-generation copy.
- Use evidence, real anonymized work, diagrams, charts and decision frameworks whenever possible.
- The company should sound like a consultancy with judgment, not a social-media content machine.
- No invented experience, benchmarks, client stories or outcomes.
- No exaggerated claims about AI replacing people or transforming everything.

The company may describe capabilities using "we" only where the statement is true for SC-Analytics as an organization. Do not use plural language to imply a larger permanent team than actually exists.
