# Forbidden / Discouraged Language

Avoid generic consultancy and AI language unless the phrase is genuinely necessary and made specific immediately.

Discouraged phrases:

- leverage
- unlock value
- unlock insights
- digital transformation
- data-driven (as an empty adjective)
- best-in-class
- cutting-edge
- revolutionary
- game-changing
- end-to-end (without defining the endpoints)
- scalable solution (without explaining what scales and why)
- in today's fast-paced environment
- companies increasingly recognize
- the power of AI
- AI-powered transformation
- seamless integration
- optimize everything

Never write:

- invented client quotes
- invented direct experience
- unsupported percentages or ROI claims
- fake urgency
- guaranteed outcomes
- vague "we have helped many companies" claims unless the underlying evidence exists

Prefer concrete nouns, named decisions, named systems, actual constraints and measurable business mechanisms.
