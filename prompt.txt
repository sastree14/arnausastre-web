SC-ANALYTICS – CONTACT PAGE CONVERSION REFINEMENT

Objective:

Refine the Contact page so that it feels easier, warmer and less demanding for a potential client to start a conversation.

The goal is not to collect a complete project brief.

The goal is to make the visitor think:

"I lose nothing by contacting them."

The page should reduce friction and make the first step feel simple, professional and low-risk.

Maintain the existing SC-Analytics visual identity:

- Elegant
- Minimalist
- Professional
- Executive
- Trustworthy
- Calm

Do not redesign the entire website.

Focus only on the Contact page.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
1. MAIN MESSAGE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The page should clearly communicate:

The first conversation has no commitment.

Its purpose is simply to understand the situation, answer initial questions and evaluate whether SC-Analytics can genuinely help.

Use this idea near the top of the contact form.

Suggested wording direction:

"La primera conversación no tiene ningún compromiso.

Su único objetivo es comprender la situación, resolver dudas iniciales y valorar si podemos aportar ayuda."

You may refine the wording, but preserve the meaning.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
2. SIMPLIFY THE CONTACT FORM
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Reduce the form to the minimum necessary fields.

Use only:

- Nombre
- Empresa or Organización (optional)
- Email
- Mensaje

Remove the current "Tipo de proyecto" / service selector.

Reason:

We do not want visitors to choose a predefined service before we understand the situation.

This is consistent with the SC-Analytics philosophy:

Understand before building.

Technology and services come later.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
3. MESSAGE FIELD
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Rename or guide the message field so it feels conversational.

Suggested label:

"Situación, problema u oportunidad"

Suggested placeholder:

"Describe brevemente qué te gustaría mejorar, qué problema quieres resolver o qué oportunidad te gustaría explorar. No hace falta entrar en detalles; la primera conversación sirve precisamente para entender el contexto."

The message field should invite a short, simple explanation.

It should not feel like a formal project brief.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
4. CTA BUTTON
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Replace the current button text:

"Enviar mensaje"

with a more consultative CTA.

Preferred option:

"Iniciar conversación"

Alternative acceptable option:

"Solicitar primera conversación"

The CTA should feel calm and professional, not aggressive.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
5. RIGHT-SIDE INFORMATION PANEL
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Simplify the current contact information panel.

Keep only practical information:

- Email
- LinkedIn
- Expected response time

Add:

"Respuesta habitual: 24–48 horas"

Remove or reduce the sections:

- Especialización
- Forma de trabajar

Reason:

These ideas are already explained throughout the website.

The Contact page should focus on making the next step easy.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
6. GUIDANCE SECTION
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Keep a lightweight guidance section, but make it less demanding.

Current structure feels like instructions for completing a business analysis.

Instead, make it feel like optional help.

Title suggestion:

"Algunas preguntas que pueden ayudarte"

Include 4 short prompts:

- ¿Qué situación te gustaría mejorar?
- ¿Qué decisión te gustaría tomar con mayor confianza?
- ¿Qué limitación está afectando actualmente al negocio?
- ¿Existe alguna oportunidad que crees que no estás aprovechando?

Important:

Make clear that answering all of these is not required.

Suggested supporting text:

"No es necesario responder a todo. Una breve explicación es suficiente para iniciar la conversación."

──────────────────────────────────────────────
7. TONE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The tone should be:

- Simple
- Trustworthy
- Consultative
- Low-pressure
- Professional

Avoid:

- Sales language
- "Book a demo"
- "Schedule your strategy session"
- Aggressive conversion language
- Overly technical wording

This is not a sales funnel.

This is the beginning of a professional conversation.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
8. FINAL OBJECTIVE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The Contact page should make it very easy for a CEO, founder or operations manager to reach out even if they do not yet know exactly how to define the project.

The visitor should feel:

- I do not need to know the solution.
- I do not need to write a perfect brief.
- I can simply explain the situation.
- The first conversation is without commitment.
- SC-Analytics will help me understand whether there is value in moving forward.
