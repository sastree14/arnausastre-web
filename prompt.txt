SC-ANALYTICS HOMEPAGE REFINEMENT

Before making any modifications, review the project context and positioning already implemented across the website.

The goal of this update is NOT to redesign the homepage.

The goal is to refine the existing homepage so that it better reflects the strategic positioning of SC-Analytics.

Maintain the current visual identity, typography, spacing philosophy, premium consultancy feel and minimalist aesthetic.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
1. ALTERNATING SECTION BACKGROUNDS
──────────────────────────────────────────────

I want a clearer visual rhythm throughout the homepage.

Sections should alternate between:

- Dark Navy Background
- Light Background

whenever possible.

Target structure:

1. Hero → Dark
2. Business Challenges → Light
3. How We Work → Dark
4. Disciplines Framework → Light
5. Philosophy → Dark
6. Applications / Use Cases → Light
7. Hidden Opportunities in Data → Dark
8. Final CTA → Light

The objective is to improve visual hierarchy and create a more elegant executive reading experience.

Avoid long sequences of sections sharing the same background color.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
2. REMOVE THE SECTION:
"EMPEZAMOS POR LA DECISIÓN, NO POR LA TECNOLOGÍA"
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Remove the entire section beginning with:

"Empezamos por la decisión, no por la tecnología."

including:

- the title
- the supporting text
- the 5 process cards
- the related CTA

Reason:

The same idea is already communicated by:

"No empezamos por la tecnología. Empezamos por el problema."

The homepage currently repeats the same concept too many times.

Removing this section will improve clarity and reduce redundancy.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
3. REWORK THE APPLICATIONS / USE CASES SECTION
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Current title:

"Un mismo enfoque puede aplicarse a situaciones muy diferentes."

Keep the title.

Keep the supporting message.

However, replace the current cards completely.

The current cards feel too service-oriented and too specific.

The new cards should be generic enough to apply to virtually any industry, company size or business context.

Create 6 cards instead of 3.

Suggested structure:

01
Crecimiento

Tomar decisiones con mayor control a medida que aumenta la complejidad del negocio.

02
Eficiencia

Reducir desperdicios, mejorar procesos y utilizar mejor los recursos disponibles.

03
Planificación

Anticipar escenarios futuros y prepararse para diferentes posibilidades.

04
Coordinación

Alinear información, equipos y procesos para mejorar la ejecución.

05
Control

Comprender qué está ocurriendo realmente y detectar desviaciones antes.

06
Oportunidades

Identificar patrones, relaciones y posibilidades de mejora que todavía no son visibles.

The cards should visually match the existing design language.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
4. INTRODUCE THE "DISCOVERING OPPORTUNITIES" NARRATIVE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Currently the homepage focuses heavily on:

- solving problems
- reducing uncertainty
- improving decisions

This should remain the primary narrative.

However, there is an important secondary narrative that is currently missing:

Many organisations already possess valuable information but are not yet extracting its full potential.

SC-Analytics should not only help solve known problems.

SC-Analytics should also help discover opportunities that are currently hidden within data, processes and operations.

This concept should be introduced subtly across multiple sections.

Do NOT make it sound like a BI company.

Do NOT make it sound like a dashboard company.

Do NOT use startup-style language.

Do NOT use buzzwords.

Instead position it as:

"understanding more"

"seeing patterns"

"identifying opportunities"

"finding relationships"

"improving decisions"

──────────────────────────────────────────────
5. UPDATE THE HERO SUPPORTING TEXT
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Current message focuses on understanding the problem before proposing a solution.

Keep that philosophy.

However introduce a subtle reference to discovering opportunities.

Example direction:

We understand the problem before proposing a solution.

We design solutions aligned with the reality of each organisation.

We also help uncover opportunities that often remain hidden within operational processes and business information.

Do not use this wording literally.

Create a better version consistent with the website tone.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
6. UPDATE THE BUSINESS CHALLENGES SECTION
──────────────────────────────────────────────

The current challenge cards focus mostly on problems.

Add one additional challenge card.

Suggested topic:

"Oportunidades desaprovechadas"

Description direction:

Valuable information exists within the organisation but is not being used to improve decisions, anticipate outcomes or identify improvement opportunities.

Keep wording concise.

Maintain consistency with existing cards.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
7. UPDATE THE DISCIPLINES FRAMEWORK
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Keep the current disciplines:

- Visualización
- Modelización
- Forecasting
- Optimización
- Automatización

Do not change the structure.

Do not change the visual concept.

However revise descriptions so they subtly communicate that these disciplines help not only solve problems but also reveal opportunities and relationships that are not immediately visible.

Examples of direction:

Visualización:
Understanding what is happening and identifying patterns that may otherwise go unnoticed.

Modelización:
Understanding how systems behave and revealing relationships that are not immediately obvious.

Forecasting:
Anticipating future scenarios and recognising opportunities before they materialise.

Optimización:
Discovering better alternatives and more efficient ways of operating.

Keep descriptions concise and elegant.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
8. ADD A NEW SECTION:
"LO QUE TUS DATOS YA SABEN"
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Insert a completely new section after:

"Un mismo enfoque puede aplicarse a situaciones muy diferentes."

and before the final CTA.

Background:
Dark Navy

Title:

"Lo que tus datos ya saben."

Subtitle direction:

Every organisation generates information continuously.

Sales.
Operations.
Production.
Customers.
Resources.
Processes.

Many companies use data only to describe what has already happened.

Often there is much more value hidden beneath the surface.

The purpose of this section is NOT to sell AI.

The purpose is NOT to sell dashboards.

The purpose is to communicate that valuable business knowledge is often already present inside the organisation.

Create four cards:

01
Detectar oportunidades

02
Comprender patrones

03
Anticipar riesgos

04
Encontrar palancas de valor

Write concise descriptions for each.

Maintain the premium consultancy tone.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
9. FINAL CTA
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Keep the existing CTA concept.

The current CTA is strong.

Only make adjustments if necessary to improve consistency after introducing the new section.

The CTA should remain:

calm
professional
consultative
non-salesy

The objective is to invite a conversation, not push for a conversion.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
10. IMPORTANT POSITIONING RULES
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Do not position SC-Analytics as:

- an AI agency
- a software development company
- a dashboard provider
- a data visualization company

Always position SC-Analytics as:

A consultancy that designs and implements Decision Systems.

Technology is a tool.

Business decisions are the objective.

Everything on the homepage should reinforce:

"Better decisions lead to better business outcomes."
