SC-ANALYTICS – CREATE THE "SOBRE NOSOTROS" PAGE

IMPORTANT

Create a completely new "Sobre nosotros" page.

Do not approach this page as a personal biography.

Do not create a traditional "About Me" page.

Do not focus on academic credentials, technologies, programming languages or personal history.

The purpose of this page is to explain:

* Why SC-Analytics exists.
* What principles guide our work.
* What kind of problems we help organisations solve.
* What vision we have for the future.

The page should feel consistent with both the homepage and the "Cómo trabajamos" page.

Maintain the same visual language:

* Elegant
* Minimalist
* Professional
* Executive
* High-trust
* Premium consultancy

Alternate dark and light sections as done throughout the website.

Avoid startup aesthetics, marketing language and exaggerated claims.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 – HERO
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Title:

Sobre nosotros

The hero should communicate that SC-Analytics was created around a simple idea:

Better decisions require more than technology.

They require understanding, analysis and a rigorous understanding of reality.

The hero should establish trust and clearly connect with the philosophy already presented on the homepage.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 – WHY SC-ANALYTICS EXISTS
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Title suggestion:

Por qué existe SC-Analytics

Purpose:

Explain that many organisations generate valuable information every day but struggle to transform it into better decisions.

Explain that SC-Analytics exists to help bridge the gap between:

* Data and action
* Information and decisions
* Analysis and implementation

The focus should be on helping organisations operate with greater clarity, control and confidence.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 – OUR WAY OF THINKING
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Title suggestion:

Nuestra forma de pensar

Present the principles that guide every recommendation and every project.

Suggested principles:

* Comprender antes de construir
* Tecnología cuando aporta valor
* Honestidad profesional
* Soluciones proporcionadas
* Valor empresarial antes que complejidad

Important:

Do not present them as slogans.

Explain what they mean in practice and how they influence decisions.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 – WHAT WE HELP ORGANISATIONS IMPROVE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

IMPORTANT

Do NOT present this as a list of services.

Do NOT use words such as:

* Forecasting
* Machine Learning
* Optimisation
* Automation

as the primary message.

Instead focus on outcomes and business challenges.

Examples:

* Reducir incertidumbre
* Mejorar la calidad de las decisiones
* Optimizar el uso de recursos
* Incrementar la visibilidad operativa
* Detectar oportunidades ocultas
* Mejorar la planificación
* Convertir información en acción

The visitor should recognise their challenges, not our tools.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 – OUR VISION
──────────────────────────────────────────────

This should be one of the most important sections.

Use the following ideas as inspiration and preserve their meaning:

"Creemos que las mejores decisiones surgen cuando se combina experiencia, análisis y una comprensión rigurosa de la realidad.

Nuestro objetivo es seguir desarrollando sistemas que ayuden a las organizaciones a operar con mayor control, claridad y confianza.

A medida que SC-Analytics evolucione, mantendremos el mismo principio que guía nuestro trabajo desde el inicio:

Comprender antes de construir."

Expand and refine this message while maintaining its essence.

Important:

Do not talk about becoming a large company.

Do not talk about growth for the sake of growth.

Focus on quality, trust, impact and long-term value.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
FINAL CTA
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Create a final section consistent with the rest of the website.

The CTA should feel consultative rather than sales-oriented.

Possible direction:

Cada organización es diferente.

Si quieres compartir una situación, problema u oportunidad, estaremos encantados de conocerla y analizarla contigo.

Keep it elegant, professional and aligned with the philosophy of SC-Analytics.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
IMPORTANT FINAL NOTE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

This page should not feel like a founder profile.

It should feel like the presentation of a consultancy and the philosophy that guides it.

The objective is to build trust, credibility and clarity.
