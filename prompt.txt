You are responsible for designing and implementing the complete editorial system for SC-Analytics.

Context

SC-Analytics is not a content marketing company.

SC-Analytics helps organizations make better business decisions through forecasting, optimization, automation, analytics and decision systems.

The objective of the editorial system is not to generate SEO content at scale.

The objective is to build a high-quality business knowledge library that reinforces expertise, credibility and trust.

Every design decision must prioritize long-term maintainability, editorial quality and business relevance.

---

EDITORIAL DATABASE

The file:

SC_Analytics_Editorial_System_Final.xlsx

is the editorial source of truth.

The workbook contains:

* Content Pipeline
* Topics Bank
* Industries
* Prompts
* Published Content
* Content Strategy
* Projects

All editorial workflows must read and update this workbook.

When an article is generated, metadata must be updated inside the workbook.

When an article is published, the workbook must be updated.

The workbook is the operational control layer.

---

CONTENT STORAGE

Do not store articles inside large TypeScript arrays.

Create a scalable architecture.

Use:

content/articles/

Each article must be stored as an individual MDX file.

Each article must contain metadata including:

* title
* slug
* industry
* challenge
* audience
* level
* theme
* date
* readingTime
* published
* language

The website must automatically discover articles from this folder.

The architecture must support hundreds of articles.

---

BRANCH STRATEGY

Do not create one branch per article.

Use a single branch:

editorial

Workflow:

Excel
→ Article generation
→ Commit to editorial
→ Vercel preview
→ Manual review
→ Manual merge to main
→ Production

Publication must always require manual approval.

Never publish directly to production.

EMPTY STATES

If a filter combination contains no articles:

show a professional empty state.

Example:

No articles available yet.

We are currently expanding our knowledge base in this area.
New content will be published soon.

Never show blank sections.

---

PROJECTS

Projects must follow the same architectural principles.

Use:

content/projects/

Projects are not blog posts.

Projects are practical examples of implemented systems.

Initially support:

* Forecasting & Inventory Optimization
* Banking Risk Analytics
* Investment Analytics Platform
* Quantitative Trading Framework
* Business Intelligence & Decision Systems

The architecture must support future growth.

---

EDITORIAL STYLE

This is the most important requirement.

Articles must not sound AI-generated.

Avoid:

* generic AI wording
* startup buzzwords
* exaggerated claims
* sensationalism
* unnecessary hype

Writing style should be:

* calm
* analytical
* precise
* practical
* business-oriented
* honest
* explanatory

The goal is to help readers understand a problem and possible approaches.

Not to impress them with technology.

Technology should appear only when it contributes to business outcomes.

The writing must reflect the philosophy:

Better decisions.
Better business outcomes.

---

STYLE LEARNING

Create a system capable of learning writing style from examples.

If writing samples from Arnau Sastre are available:

* analyze tone
* analyze vocabulary
* analyze sentence structure
* analyze explanation patterns
* create an Editorial Voice Profile

Future articles should follow this profile.

The objective is not to imitate perfectly.

The objective is to preserve consistency and authenticity.

---

IMPLEMENTATION

Before making changes:

1. Analyze current architecture.
2. Explain proposed modifications.
3. List files that will be changed.
4. Implement incrementally.
5. Run build validation.
6. Ensure scalability.
7. Ensure maintainability.
8. Preserve existing design quality.
