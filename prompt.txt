SC-ANALYTICS HOMEPAGE – VISUAL DIFFERENTIATION REFINEMENT

The homepage content is now largely correct.

The next objective is not to add more content.

The objective is to improve visual storytelling and reduce repetition.

Currently several sections follow exactly the same structure:

- Large title
- Small subtitle
- Grid of cards

Although the content is different, the visual experience begins to feel repetitive.

I want each major section to communicate its idea through a different visual structure while preserving the minimalist, executive and premium consultancy aesthetic.

Do not redesign the website.

Do not introduce animations.

Do not introduce modern startup aesthetics.

Maintain the current SC-Analytics visual identity.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PHILOSOPHY SECTION
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Section title:

"Soluciones sencillas para problemas complejos."

Remove completely the three supporting lines below the title:

- Comprender antes de construir.
- Tecnología cuando aporta valor.
- Decisiones mejores, sistemas más sólidos.

Reason:

The cards already communicate these principles.

The supporting text creates unnecessary repetition.

The section should feel more confident and minimalist.

Desired structure:

Title

Directly below:
4 philosophy cards

No additional explanatory text.

Allow the cards to carry the message.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
4. IMPROVE VISUAL EMPHASIS OF SUBTITLES
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Several sections contain supporting text beneath large titles.

Currently these subtitles visually disappear because:

- typography hierarchy is weak
- spacing is too uniform
- titles dominate excessively

Review subtitle styling throughout the homepage.

Without making them larger, improve readability through:

- spacing
- alignment
- line length
- hierarchy
- contrast

The supporting message should be easy to read and clearly intentional.

Avoid large blocks of text.

Keep everything concise.
